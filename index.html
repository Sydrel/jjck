Our Story

The Ultimate Card Shop In the Treasure Coast!

Welcome to JJCK Collectibles, the Treasure Coast’s premier family-owned sports card and collectibles shop!

We specialize in a wide range of trading cards and collectibles, including Topps, Panini, Pokémon, One Piece, Magic: The Gathering, Lorcana, and more. Whether you're a passionate sports fan, TCG player, or pop culture enthusiast, JJCK Collectibles offers something for every type of collector.

Located in a welcoming, family-friendly environment, our knowledgeable team is here to help beginners and seasoned collectors alike find the perfect additions to their collections.

At JJCK Collectibles, we’re more than just a shop — we’re a community built on a shared love for collecting. Stop by and experience the difference today!
